## CDC VSRR provisional overdose deaths, county (`cdc_vsrr_overdose_county.csv`)

Source: data.cdc.gov dataset gb4e-yj24.

- Rows: 226,368; columns (17): `Data as of`, `Year`, `Month`, `ST_ABBREV`, `STATE_NAME`, `COUNTYNAME`, `FIPS`, `STATEFIPS`, `COUNTYFIPS`, `CODE2013`, `Provisional Drug Overdose Deaths`, `Footnote`, `Percentage Of Records Pending Investigation`, `HistoricalDataCompletenessNote`, `MonthEndingDate`, `Start Date`, `End Date`
- `Data as of`: ['07/05/2026']
- Month-ending dates: 2020-01 to 2025-12 (72 months);
  rows per month: 3144–3144
- Distinct `FIPS`: 3,142; states (`ST_ABBREV`): 51
- **Duplicate FIPS within a month**: two FIPS appear twice per month (Virginia independent cities
  folded into their counties), with identical values. Deduplicate before summing or joining.
  Example, 2023-12:

| ST_ABBREV | COUNTYNAME | FIPS | Provisional Drug Overdose Deaths |
|---|---|---|---|
| VA | Alleghany | 51005 | 14 |
| VA | Clifton Forge (city) | 51005 | 14 |
| VA | Bedford (city) | 51019 | 38 |
| VA | Bedford | 51019 | 38 |

- **No drug/indicator column.** This file is all-drug overdose deaths only; it cannot give opioid-specific county counts.
- **Values are 12-month-ending totals**, like the state file. Check: summing every county for 2023-12
  (one row per FIPS) gives 99,691, versus 106,881 for `US` / `Number of Drug Overdose Deaths` / 2023-12 in the state file.
  The shortfall is consistent with suppressed cells.
- Suppressed/missing `Provisional Drug Overdose Deaths`: 47.0% of rows
  (footnote: counts 1–9 suppressed under NCHS confidentiality rules).
- `CODE2013` (NCHS 2013 urban–rural code, 1 = most urban … 6 = most rural) row counts: {'1': 4896, '2': 26496, '3': 26856, '4': 25776, '5': 46152, '6': 96192}
- `Footnote` values: ['One or more data cells have counts between 1-9 and have been suppressed in accordance with NCHS confidentiality standards.']
- `HistoricalDataCompletenessNote` values: ['Completeness of provisional 2019 data under 90% after 6 months.']

### Join key (FIPS) against SVI 2022

- `FIPS` is stored **without leading zeros** (e.g. `1001`); zero-pad to 5 characters before joining.
- After padding, in county file but not SVI: ['02261', '09001', '09003', '09005', '09007', '09009', '09011', '09013', '09015']
- In SVI but not county file: ['02063', '02066', '09110', '09120', '09130', '09140', '09150', '09160', '09170', '09180', '09190']
- Connecticut: the county file uses the 8 legacy counties (09001–09015); SVI 2022 uses the 9 planning
  regions (09110–09190). Alaska: 02261 (Valdez-Cordova) vs 02063/02066 (Chugach/Copper River).
  These do not join without a crosswalk.
